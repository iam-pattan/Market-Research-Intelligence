# Banyan outreach playbook — thesis, voice, compliance

## Thesis fit (which angle to use)

**banyan-fit (Permanent-Home acquisition motion):**
- Vertical / enterprise B2B software leading a defined niche
- ~$2M–$100M revenue, profitable with positive cash flow
- High recurring-revenue contribution, strong retention
- Founder- / family-owned or bootstrapped; NOT recently PE/VC-recapped
- Owner values legacy / permanence over a maximized exit

**growth-thesis (NOT a buy-and-hold target):**
- VC/PE-backed, scaling, often unprofitable or >$100M
- These are relationship/awareness contacts at most; never pitch permanent-home acquisition.
- Route to a growth mandate if one exists, else do-not-contact.

## Brand voice

- Calm, respectful, founder-to-founder. Never salesy, never hypey.
- Differentiator is **permanence** (buy-and-hold-forever) vs. the PE flip. Lead with that.
- Concrete proof: "120+ software companies acquired, held permanently, zero sold" (self-reported — see caveat).
- Respect the legacy: brand, team, mission preserved. The emotional core, not the check size.
- Low-commitment CTA: one short, time-boxed, optional call.

## Compliance / hygiene do's & don'ts

- **Do NOT** quote the recipient any revenue/margin/TPV figure — those are internal estimates, often Confidential; stating them signals surveillance and may be wrong.
- **Do NOT** imply you hold non-public information about them (MNPI hygiene). Personalize only from public, verifiable details.
- **Do NOT** manufacture urgency or scarcity — it backfires with founders and misrepresents a buy-and-hold acquirer.
- **Do** keep one sender, plain text, real signature; no tracking-pixel feel.
- **Gate:** lead+pitch content is Sales/BD-only. Enforce via `banyan_screen.policy.guard(role, ...)`. Data Science / Finance never receive it.

## Caveat to carry

Banyan's "120+ acquired / sold zero / founded 2016 / permanent capital" figures are self-reported from banyansoftware.com and were not third-party verified. They are fine as positioning; do not present them as audited.
