# Outreach templates & frameworks

Placeholders: `{first_name}`, `{company}`, `{niche}`, `{specific_detail}`, `{sender}`, `{sender_title}`.
Keep bodies 50–125 words. One CTA. Plain text.

---

## 1. Permanent-Home Acquisition (PRIMARY — banyan-fit leads)

**Subject:** `{niche} software` · or a genuine observation ("Your {niche} traction")

```
Hi {first_name},

{specific_detail} — you've built {company} into a real leader in {niche}, which is exactly why I'm reaching out.

I'm with Banyan Software. We acquire vertical software companies and hold them permanently — no fund clock, no resale. We've done this with 120+ businesses and sold zero; founders come to us when they want a lasting home that keeps the brand, team, and mission they built intact.

No agenda beyond a conversation — would you be open to a 20-minute call in the next couple of weeks to compare notes on {niche}?

Best,
{sender}
{sender_title}, Banyan Software
```

---

## 2. Founder-Succession angle (banyan-fit, owner nearing transition)

**Subject:** `A permanent home for {company}`

```
Hi {first_name},

{specific_detail}. Companies like {company} are rare — a profitable, well-run leader in a niche most people never see.

Most acquirers optimize for an exit. Banyan is the opposite: we buy and hold forever, keep your team and brand, and let the business keep operating the way you built it. 120+ companies acquired, none ever sold.

If you ever think about what's next for {company} — even years out — it's worth knowing a permanent-home option exists. Open to a short call?

{sender}
```

---

## 3. PAS (Problem–Agitate–Solve) — for owners weighing PE offers

**Subject:** `Beyond the PE playbook`

```
Hi {first_name},

Most offers a founder in {niche} gets come from PE firms that will flip {company} in 3–5 years — new management, cost cuts, another sale. (Problem)
That's a hard legacy to hand your team and customers after building {specific_detail}. (Agitate)
Banyan is built the other way: permanent ownership, brand and team preserved, no resale clock — 120+ companies held, zero sold. (Solve)

Worth a 20-minute conversation, even just for context?

{sender}
```

---

## 4. Follow-up (no reply, +7–10 days)

**Subject:** `re: {niche} software`

```
Hi {first_name}, floating this back up. No pressure at all — if a permanent home for {company} is ever relevant, even down the road, I'm happy to be a resource. Would a short call in the next few weeks work? If now's not the time, just say the word and I'll circle back later in the year.

{sender}
```

---

## 5. Growth-thesis lead (NOT an acquisition target)

Do **not** send a permanent-home pitch. If a growth mandate exists, use a relationship note:

```
Hi {first_name}, {specific_detail} — impressive momentum in {niche}. I follow the space and wanted to connect; no agenda beyond staying in touch as {company} scales.
```
Otherwise mark `do-not-contact` and route internally. Never imply acquisition interest you can't back.
