**Aikido Security, as of September 2026, looks like a high-growth, VC-backed European AppSec platform moving from "developer-first security consolidation" into "AI-driven self-securing software."** It is probably more of a strategic-growth target than a cash-flow acquisition target today.

**Business model and product:** Aikido sells a SaaS software security platform for engineering and security teams. Its packaging includes a free developer tier, paid team plans, and enterprise/custom modules. The platform bundles SCA, SAST, secrets detection, license/SBOM, IaC/cloud/container scanning, DAST/API scanning, runtime protection, bot/device protection, compliance reporting, Jira/Linear/GRC integrations, and AI-driven pentesting/remediation. Public pricing shows a free tier and a Basic plan starting at $300/month, with broader modules quoted or custom.

**Vertical niche and market position:** Its core niche is developer-first Application Security Posture Management and AppSec consolidation for SaaS, cloud-native, and fast-shipping engineering teams. Aikido's wedge is "less noise, fewer tools, faster fixes" versus buying separate Snyk, Wiz, Veracode, Checkmarx, Semgrep, GitHub Advanced Security, Socket, Mend, etc. The company claims 150k+ teams protected, 50k+ organizations, customers across 70+ countries, and named customers/teams such as Revolut, Deel, SoundCloud, Premier League, Tines, Niantic, n8n, Visma, Legora, and others. Its January 2026 $1B valuation makes it one of Europe's fastest cybersecurity unicorns.

**Revenue / ARR and profitability signals:** No audited revenue, ARR, EBITDA, or burn figures appear public. The 2026 Aikido Infinite launch is particularly strategic: autonomous agents pentest every meaningful release, validate exploitability, generate fixes, and retest.
