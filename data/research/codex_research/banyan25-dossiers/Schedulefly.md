## Answer

**Schedulefly snapshot:** Schedulefly is a small, bootstrapped restaurant employee scheduling and communications SaaS company based in North Carolina, legally associated publicly with Fly Holdings, LLC. It was created in 2007 and focuses on independent restaurants, bars, breweries, coffee shops, boutique hotels, and other hospitality operators.

**Business model & product:** Subscription SaaS, flat monthly pricing by staff count, with no contracts, no setup fee, no per-user pricing, and a 30-day free trial. Public pricing is $30/month for up to 19 staff, $40/month for 20-39, $50/month for 40-59, $60/month for 60-79, and quote-based for 80+ staff. Product modules include schedule creation/publishing, SMS/email alerts, shift trades with approval, time-off requests, mobile access, staff messaging, labor-cost reporting, activity reporting, company messages/documents, and "Fly Notes."

**Vertical niche & market position:** It is tightly verticalized around hospitality labor scheduling, especially independent restaurants rather than enterprise chains. The company says it has served 20,000+ restaurants since 2007 and has 5,000+ currently on Schedulefly. A 2024 app-launch release said the mobile app served 5,200+ restaurants and 225,000+ app users across the U.S. and Canada. This makes it meaningful in its niche, but not scaled like broader workforce platforms such as 7shifts, HotSchedules, Homebase, When I Work, Deputy, Connecteam, Sling, and Restaurant365.

**Revenue / ARR estimate:** Public data is inconsistent. GetLatka estimates 2025 revenue at $660K ARR, 6 employees, $0 funding, and a $2M valuation. However, Schedulefly's own current claim of 5,000+ active restaurants and its published minimum price of $30/month imply a theoretical ARR floor of roughly **$1.8M** if those are paying locations. At common listed tiers, ARR could plausibly be **$2M-$4M+**, before any 80+ staff quotes. For M&A purposes, I would underwrite a diligence range, not a point estimate: **$0.7M third-party modeled ARR vs. $1.8M+ pricing-derived ARR floor**. The discrepancy is a key diligence item.
